Accepted: support hold-to-talk and press-to-start/press-to-stop. Hold-to-talk is
the default. Let the user choose the activation shortcut. This does not choose a
factory key, promise every physical key combination on every OS, or include
wake-word activation. Platform conflicts/limitations must be disclosed and an
invalid assignment must not remove an existing working assignment.
