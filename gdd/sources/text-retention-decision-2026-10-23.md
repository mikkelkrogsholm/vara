# Text history retention — 2026-10-23

Direct user answer, copied verbatim:

> Gem historikken indtil jeg selv sletter, men lad brugeren vælge automatisk oprydning efter en periode.

Accepted: keep text history until manual deletion by default. Automatic cleanup
is optional and its retention period is chosen by the user. There is no default
age/count-based truncation. This applies to text history, not indefinite audio
retention. Raw-versus-processed text storage, audio lifecycle and exact optional
cleanup UI/period values remain separate decisions or contract refinements.
