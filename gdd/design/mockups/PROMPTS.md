Three visual proposals; none changes the accepted v1 scope. Generated with the built-in image_gen tool. Prompts below are the exact generation inputs.

## Warm precision

Filename: 01-warm-precision.png

Use case: ui-mockup.
Create a very high-fidelity, exceptionally polished desktop APPLICATION UI design mockup for "vara", a cross-platform speech dictation app. It must feel like a real expensive, carefully crafted productivity application, simple and immediately intuitive. Show one large crisp straight-on app window, nearly filling a landscape 16:10 canvas, on a very restrained background. No laptop, phone, hands, perspective tilt or marketing website. Prioritize actual usable interface, excellent typographic hierarchy, disciplined spacing, subtle alignment, refined icons, beautiful material restraint. All UI copy Danish, legible and clean.

Product constraints: three main destinations "Historik", "AI pr. app", "Indstillinger". Main view is saved dictation history grouped by the application where dictation started. Originating apps: "Mail", "Noter", "Visual Studio Code". Local and direct transcription default. A processed result shows finished text first with collapsed "Vis original" disclosure. One deliberate "Kopiér" action. Selected completed text: "Hej Anna. Tak for din mail. Jeg kan godt torsdag kl. 10. Send gerne en invitation." Example earlier notes: "Vi starter med den enkle løsning." and "Tilføj en tydelig fejlbesked, hvis transskriptionen fejler." Keep content sparse and readable. Small quiet status "På enheden" and illustrative shortcut "⌥ Space". An unobtrusive separate small floating dictation HUD near bottom shows waveform, "Lytter" and "Mail", as an example of the recording surface, not a huge primary recording button. App is a quiet utility; avoid dashboard metrics, charts, gradients-as-decoration, oversized rounded cards, onboarding marketing headlines, subscription upsells, AI sparkle overload, excessive controls or clutter. Use realistic sizes and a balanced readable information density. No features beyond dictation/history/app prompts/settings.
Art direction 1 — WARM PRECISION. Light warm alabaster canvas, deep olive-ink narrow sidebar, tiny burnt-sienna accents. Quiet tactile native desktop craftsmanship, fine hairline separators, softly rounded 10px corners, matte panels, no excessive shadows. Three-column layout: elegant compact navigation sidebar; central history grouped by originating app with selected Mail item; right reading pane with generous readable text and a fine original disclosure below. Small polished custom waveform brand mark beside lowercase vara. Sophisticated typographic rhythm, human and warm while precise. Restrained orange copy action, not multiple competing accents. The result must look noticeably more art-directed and premium than a generic component-library template.
