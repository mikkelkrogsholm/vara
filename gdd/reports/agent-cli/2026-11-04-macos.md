
The real inference command used an isolated profile, the installed launcher and
pre-existing local Redux and Who weights:

```sh

  "$HOME/Library/Application Support/dk.vara.desktop/models" \
  /tmp/vara29-real-final "$HOME/.local/bin/vara" > /tmp/vara29-real-final.log 2>&1
```
